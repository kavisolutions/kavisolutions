# Kavi Fellowship — 11-month curriculum

Working curriculum for the first cohort. It is a plan, not a promise: the
execution team should revise it after each cohort and record what actually
worked.

## Design principles

1. **Practice before theory.** Every module ends with something the fellow
   built, ran, wrote or presented.
2. **One cohort, one rhythm.** Vipassana, YLTP and DSN are woven through the
   year, not isolated workshops.
3. **Track depth, shared breadth.** All fellows learn communication, digital
   literacy and community practice; each track goes deep on its craft.
4. **Evidence over attendance.** Progress is judged by deliverables, reviews
   and a public capstone.
5. **Honest outcomes.** No job guarantee, no salary promise and no certificate
   without completion.

## Month-by-month plan

### Month 1 — Arrive and reset

- Onboarding, cohort agreements, safety and safeguarding.
- Vipassana preparation and the ten-day course.
- Digital literacy baseline: email, documents, spreadsheets, calendars,
  passwords and device hygiene.
- Communication: listening, writing a clear update, asking for help.
- Baseline skills audit and personal learning plan.
- First tiny build: a personal page or a one-page research brief.

### Month 2 — Foundation and track choice

- English and Telugu communication practice.
- Problem framing, user research basics and evidence.
- Track exposure rotations: technology, research, marketing, operations.
- Track choice and mentor group assignment.
- Team project: a small, real brief with a demo at the end of the month.
- YLTP leadership lab 1: facilitating a conversation.

### Month 3 — Craft I

- Track-specific core skills.
- Technology: HTML/CSS/JS, Git, React basics, APIs.
- Research: questionnaire design, sampling basics, field logistics.
- Marketing: audience, messaging, content formats, analytics basics.
- Operations: project tracking, documentation, spreadsheets, basic accounts.
- Weekly critique habit; written action lists after every review.
- DSN community immersion 1: listen before proposing anything.

### Month 4 — Craft II

- Technology: TypeScript, database basics, authentication, testing.
- Research: data cleaning, descriptive analysis, report structure.
- Marketing: campaign planning, copy, design constraints, measurement.
- Operations: budgeting, vendor coordination, reporting cadence.
- YLTP leadership lab 2: mobilising a team and handling conflict.
- Mid-track project review with an external practitioner.

### Month 5 — Craft III and first live brief

- Technology: deployment, debugging, code review, performance basics.
- Research: field quality control, back-checks, dashboards.
- Marketing: channel execution, community, response handling.
- Operations: process design, risk registers, stakeholder updates.
- DSN community project proposal with a named community partner.
- First live brief begins; scope and acceptance criteria written down.

### Month 6 — Practice I

- Live project delivery with a named deliverable.
- Client communication: status updates, scope changes, escalation.
- Vipassana refresher and daily sits.
- DSN community project execution.
- YLTP leadership lab 3: running a public-facing session.
- Mid-programme review: what to keep, what to change, what to stop.

### Month 7 — Practice II

- Deeper live work; fellows take ownership of a workstream.
- Technology: integration, observability, incident basics.
- Research: analysis, visualisation, executive summary.
- Marketing: campaign optimisation, reporting, community response.
- Operations: reconciliation, documentation, handover.
- DSN reflection: power, privilege and responsible service.

### Month 8 — Practice III and specialisation choice

- Complete the live project and hand it over.
- Choose a specialisation inside the track.
- Portfolio case study draft.
- Mock client review and feedback.
- DSN community project close-out and public note.
- YLTP leadership lab 4: presenting to a room.

### Month 9 — Specialise I

- Advanced track modules.
- Portfolio rewrite with evidence and outcomes.
- Interview practice: technical, case and behavioural.
- Capstone brief scoped with a mentor.
- Begin capstone build or research.

### Month 10 — Specialise II

- Capstone execution.
- CV, LinkedIn and portfolio review.
- Referral and placement conversations.
- Mock interviews with external reviewers.
- Public speaking and demo coaching.
- DSN alumni/community check-in.

### Month 11 — Capstone and launch

- Finish and present the capstone.
- Public demo day.
- Written case study and portfolio publish.
- Next-step plan: job, freelance, venture or further study.
- Certificate verification and alumni onboarding.
- Cohort retrospective and curriculum revision.

## Assessment

| Component | Weight | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Track projects | 30% | Working deliverable, review notes |
| Live project | 25% | Client acceptance, handover |
| DSN community project | 15% | Partner confirmation, reflection |
| YLTP participation | 10% | Facilitation and team feedback |
| Capstone | 20% | Demo, case study, public artefact |

Completion requires attendance, the Vipassana course, a live project
contribution and a capstone. Certificates are issued through the verifiable
certificate system.
