TERROIR & STEWARDSHIP

Restoring the degraded red loam of the Palnadu scrublands.

Polepalli sits in the rain-shadow corridor of Palnadu. Where industrial green-revolution monocultures drained aquifers and depleted microbiological topsoil, Kavi Farms applies hyper-local water catchment trenching, desilted tank recharge beds, and indigenous heirloom multi-cropping.

No synthetic nitrogen pellets. No endocrine-disrupting pesticides. We practice bio-diverse agroforestry combining native timber stands, shade pulses, and cold-hardy dryland millets. The village is our living lab; farmers are our senior research engineers.

What this is not.

We want people who respect the land and rural pace. Please review our baseline constraints before planning a visit:

  • close No room service or air conditioning: Days are dry and warm; nights are breezy and starry under open roofs.
  • close No guaranteed waterfalls: Streams flow during and after monsoons. Dry months are scorched red rock and scrub.

CLARITY FIRST

Before You Book or Inquire

Direct, honest answers about visiting our village and buying our farm yields.

Polepalli is located approximately 3.5 hours drive from Hyderabad (ORR exit via Nagarjuna Sagar highway) and 2 hours from Vijayawada / Guntur. The final 6 kilometers are single-lane tarred rural roads that lead straight to the village commons. We share exact live GPS pins upon confirmed booking.
We are not an e-commerce aggregation platform with inventory warehouses. All grains, oils, and seasonal vegetables are harvested in real-time batches based on weather and harvest maturity. Pricing covers strictly fair farmer remuneration, cold-pressing labor, and non-plastic freight costs. Inquiring lets us tell you exactly what is freshly harvested this week.
Yes, provided they enjoy authentic rural settings. Children often find the open fields, farm animals, and stone mills immensely educational. However, please note that terrain includes uneven red soil, steps, and unpaved paths. While Western toilets are fitted in the quarters, there are no medical centers within 12km, so carry essential personal prescriptions.
Meals are strictly regional Telugu vegetarian country fare prepared in clean earthen vessels. Breakfast typically features steamed millet idlis or ragi ambali. Lunches and dinners feature red gram dal, hand-harvested wild greens (thotakura, gongura), unpolished rice or jowar rotis, and country churned curds. No commercial sodas or processed junk foods are served on the premises.
