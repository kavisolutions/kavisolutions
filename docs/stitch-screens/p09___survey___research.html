Epistemic Stance

The Field Has No Patience For Polished Assumptions.

In development consulting and enterprise expansion, false precision kills initiatives before they launch. Spreadsheets filled with fabricated responses and desk-bound imputations are widespread because ground truth is exhausting to verify.

Kavi Solutions approaches field research like high-consequence systems engineering: we log every GPS transit line, verify survey duration variance down to the second, and build multi-stage audit triggers directly into survey devices. We deliver findings that stand up under hostile institutional examination.

Frequently Addressed Queries

corporate_fare CLIENT & INSTITUTIONAL FAQS

How do you prevent surveyors from faking interviews?

We enforce hardware-locked GPS coordinates per submission, random 30-second audio snippets, logic question traps, and automated speed detection. An anomalous standard deviation results in field revisit triggers.

Who owns the proprietary field data?

The client holds 100% intellectual property and database ownership upon final sign-off. Kavi Solutions holds zero secondary resale rights unless mutually codified for open public goods research.

Can you handle sensitive IRB or Ethics Board applications?

Yes. We structure consent protocols, anonymization keys, and minor-participant buffers strictly adhering to ICMR ethical guidelines and international academic IRB rubrics.

group FIELD ENUMERATOR FAQS

How and when do field enumerators get paid?

Payouts are calculated on valid, verified submissions and disbursed via Direct Bank Transfer within 7 business days of field completion and algorithmic audit clearance.

What devices and hardware are required?

You need an Android smartphone running Android 10 or above with a functioning GPS chipset and camera. For specific high-load projects, Kavi provides field tablets.

Is training provided for regional studies?

Yes. Every project begins with a paid 1 to 3 day briefing protocol, covering survey logic, respondent consent protocols, and local vernacular nuances.
