Prerequisites & Integrity

Before you brief us

Clear answers regarding how Kavi Solutions interfaces with statutory authorities, tenders, and assessment workflows.

Are you an empaneled government vendor or agency?

No. Kavi Solutions is an independent digital product and engineering studio. We do not claim official empanelment, institutional affiliation, or vendor accreditation with any state government or board. We engage through formal authorized advisory briefings or via standard statutory procurement channels.

What is required before you can assess our portal?

We require written authorization issued by a competent institutional authority (Department Head, Controller of Examinations, or Vice Chancellor) clearly outlining testing boundaries, permitted environments (preferably pre-production or mirrored testbeds), and authorized timelines.

Do you replace existing government data centres or NIC setups?

No. Our modernization frameworks focus on architectural optimization, interface decoupling, and cloud-burst caching that integrate directly with existing State Data Centres (SDC) or National Informatics Centre (NIC) cores, augmenting performance without disrupting institutional ownership.

How do you manage student and citizen data security during testing?

We insist on sanitized or synthetic data environments for diagnostic load testing. No personally identifiable citizen information (PII) is ever transferred to external systems. All technical traces and logs generated during assessments are destroyed or handed over post-briefing.
