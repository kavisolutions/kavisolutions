# Legal registration research — proprietorship, GST and FSSAI

Research notes for task #89. This is **not legal or tax advice**. Thresholds,
fees and document lists change; confirm everything with a chartered accountant
and the official portal before filing.

## 1. Proprietorship

A proprietorship is not a separate legal entity. The proprietor owns the
business, receives its income and is personally liable for its obligations.

### What to keep ready

- PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor.
- Passport-size photograph and mobile/email.
- Business name and nature of activity.
- Business address proof: rent agreement / ownership proof plus utility bill.
- Current bank account in the business name (or proprietor's name, depending on
  the bank's requirement).
- A simple letterhead, invoice format and bookkeeping process.

### Registrations commonly used with a proprietorship

| Registration | When it matters | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GST | Turnover crosses the threshold, voluntary registration, inter-state supply, e-commerce, or input-credit needs | See section 2 |
| Udyam (MSME) | Want MSME benefits, schemes or easier lending | Free online registration; not a substitute for GST |
| Shops & Establishment | State labour/establishment requirement | Rules and fees differ between Andhra Pradesh and Telangana |
| Professional tax | State-specific, based on activity and turnover | Confirm with the CA |
| Current account | Receiving business payments | Bank usually asks for two business proofs |
| Trademark | Protecting the Kavi Farms / Kavi Solutions brand | Separate application per class; use a trademark agent |

### Practical sequence

1. Confirm the business name and activity with the CA.
2. Open a current account with the proprietorship name.
3. Register for GST if required (or voluntarily).
4. Register on Udyam if benefits are useful.
5. Complete state Shops & Establishment and professional tax formalities.
6. Set up invoicing, expense records and monthly bookkeeping before the first
   sale.
7. File the first GST return and income-tax return on time.

## 2. GST registration

### Thresholds (verify current limits)

- Goods: commonly ₹40 lakh annual turnover for normal-category states.
- Services: commonly ₹20 lakh.
- Special-category states: lower limits apply.
- E-commerce sellers and certain inter-state supplies may need registration
  regardless of turnover.
- Voluntary registration is possible if it helps with input credit or B2B
  credibility.

### Documents usually requested

- PAN of the proprietor.
- Aadhaar of the proprietor.
- Photograph.
- Business address proof (rent agreement / ownership document and utility
  bill).
- Bank account proof (cancelled cheque or statement).
- Business constitution evidence (proprietorship declaration, if asked).
- Digital signature for certain entities (not usually for a proprietorship
  individual).

### Process

1. Create an account on the GST portal.
2. Fill the registration application (Form GST REG-01) and upload documents.
3. Receive the ARN and track status.
4. Respond to any clarification (Form GST REG-03) within the deadline.
5. Download the registration certificate (Form GST REG-06) and display the
   GSTIN where required.
6. Decide on the composition scheme only after checking eligibility and the
   impact on input credit.

### Ongoing compliance

- Monthly/quarterly returns based on scheme and turnover.
- Annual return.
- Invoice series, e-way bill where applicable, and reconciliation.
- Keep records for the statutory retention period.

## 3. FSSAI — relevant for Kavi Farms

FSSAI registration/licensing applies to food businesses: manufacturing,
processing, packing, storing, transporting, distributing, selling or serving
food. Farm produce sold as raw primary produce may have different treatment;
packaged products, pickles, honey, oils and tourism meals need a closer review.

### Categories

| Category | Typical scope | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Registration | Small-scale food business below the turnover threshold | Simplest form; still requires compliant labelling and hygiene |
| State Licence | Medium-sized manufacturing, storage, distribution or food service | Likely category for a farm kitchen/café and packaged products as turnover grows |
| Central Licence | Large operations, import/export, e-commerce, or specified categories | Confirm with the FSSAI consultant |

### Documents usually requested

- PAN, Aadhaar and photograph of the proprietor.
- Business address proof and premises layout (for licence).
- List of food products and categories.
- Water test report and pest-control records (for manufacturing/food service).
- Medical fitness certificates for food handlers.
- Recall plan, hygiene and sanitation SOPs (for larger categories).
- Packaging and label artwork for review.

### Process

1. Identify the correct category with the CA/FSSAI consultant.
2. Create an account on FoSCoS.
3. Submit the application and pay the fee.
4. Respond to inspection or clarification requests.
5. Display the FSSAI number on packaging, menus and the website where required.
6. Renew before expiry and keep hygiene/audit records.

### Label and menu checks

- Product name, ingredients, net quantity, date marking, storage instructions.
- FSSAI licence number and address.
- Vegetarian/non-vegetarian mark where applicable.
- Allergen and nutritional information where required.
- No health or medical claims that the product cannot substantiate.

- Do not claim certifications, government recognition or partnerships that are
  not actually held.
- Keep product claims and prices honest and current.
